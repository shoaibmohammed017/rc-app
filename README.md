# RC Car Business Manager

A complete web app to run your RC car business — sales, purchases, expenses,
inventory, customers, suppliers, staff, and a profit dashboard.

## How to open it
Double-click **`index.html`** — it opens in your web browser. That's it.
No internet, no install, no login needed. It works fully offline.

Tip: bookmark the page, or keep the `rc app` folder somewhere safe (e.g. Desktop).

## First steps
1. Open the app → go to **Settings** → click **🧪 Load sample data** to see how
   it looks with example records. (Use **🗑️ Erase everything** to start clean.)
2. Set your **Business name** and currency in Settings.
3. Add your **Staff** (the people who buy/sell/spend), **Suppliers**, and a few
   **Products** in Inventory.
4. Start recording **Sales**, **Purchases**, and **Expenses**.

## What it tracks
- **Dashboard** — revenue, net profit, gross profit, expenses, stock value,
  money to collect / money to pay, monthly trend, top products, sales by channel.
- **Sales** — what sold, qty, price, profit, channel (where sold), customer,
  *sold by whom*, payment status.
- **Purchases** — what you bought, supplier, *bought by whom*, payment status.
- **Expenses** — category, amount, *paid by whom*, payment mode.
- **Inventory** — stock auto-updates when you buy/sell; low-stock alerts.
- **Customers / Suppliers / Staff** — balances, totals, who spent what.
- **Reports** — Profit & Loss by date range, expense breakdown, spend by person,
  print/PDF.

## ⚠️ Important: back up your data
Data is stored **inside this browser on this computer**. If you clear browser
data or switch computers, it can be lost.

- Click **⬇️ Backup data** (sidebar) regularly to download a backup file.
- To restore or move to another computer: Settings → **⬆️ Restore from backup**.
- You can also export any list to **CSV** (for Excel) from its page.
